About

I hold a PhD in Mechanical and Civil Engineering from the National Polytechnic Institute of Toulouse and graduated as a general engineer from ENIT. After several years in project engineering in the Oil & Gas sector, I moved into teaching and training in Civil Engineering at ENIT, where I supervised student projects, internships and educational programmes.

From 2018 to 2022, I led the international European Project Semester, a programme built on a network of 19 European universities welcoming students from diverse academic backgrounds. Since 2022, I have taken part in the creation of UTTOP, where I now serve as Vice President in charge of International Relations.

A specialist in Building and Public Works, I combine technical expertise, project management and innovative teaching with a strong international outlook.

I am attending EAIE to build new academic partnerships, develop student and staff mobility, and strengthen UTTOP's international network.
