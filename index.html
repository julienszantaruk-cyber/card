About

As Head of Communications at UTTOP, the University of Technology of Tarbes Occitanie Pyrénées, I work at the intersection of institutional strategy, brand identity and community engagement.

I see communication as more than simply sharing information — it is a way to connect people, make ambitious projects visible and turn a shared vision into collective momentum. From institutional and internal communications to branding, content and digital strategy, my focus is on creating narratives that are clear, consistent and meaningful.

I am attending EAIE to exchange ideas with international higher education professionals, explore new perspectives and help strengthen UTTOP's visibility and connections beyond borders.
